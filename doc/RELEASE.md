[h1]PZLinux 1.0.0[/h1]

PZLinux 1.0.0 is the first release built specifically for Project Zomboid
Build 42.20 Stable. It turns the original single-player economy mod into a much
broader solo and multiplayer experience centered on banking, online commerce,
contracts and terminal-based activities.

[h2]Highlights[/h2]

[list]
[*] Added server-backed support for hosted and dedicated multiplayer while
preserving the existing single-player flow.
[*] Added six-seat Texas Hold'em Poker with AI opponents, side pots, table stakes,
hand evaluation and estimated player equity.
[*] Added Blackjack using a real 52-card deck and server-resolved bets.
[*] Reworked Zombie Race betting with animated runners and settlement at the end
of the race instead of when it starts.
[*] Rebuilt the contract and mission-location catalog for Build 42.20, growing
to 13 contract types including two new location-based contracts, Protect
the Building and Refill an ATM.
[*] Added a reputation system that rewards completed contracts and penalizes
cancellations, with tiered pricing benefits and penalties on the Dark Web
and Requests.
[*] Added survival-time scarcity: Dark Web and Request prices increase as the
world grows older, with a configurable cap after two years.
[*] Added 20 synchronized translation catalogs with matching keys and dynamic
placeholders.
[/list]

[h2]Multiplayer[/h2]

Banking, ATMs, Dark Web transactions, Trading, Contracts, Requests, Mail,
Hacking and betting now use server-side validation in multiplayer. Shared
markets, weekly contract offers and tagged world objectives are synchronized
through the server, while personal balances, reputation, orders and temporary
sessions remain attached to their player.

[list]
[*] Sensitive commands are prefixed and protected against duplicate request IDs.
[*] Contract locations, objectives and rewards are generated by the server.
[*] Mailbox and contract interactions validate the real object, distance and Z
level before changing inventory or world state.
[*] Purchased, requested and rewarded items are explicitly synchronized to the
owning client.
[*] Contract objectives remain visible in the shared world and may be stolen or
delivered by another player when their persistent tags permit it.
[*] Interrupted Blackjack, Hacking, Poker and race sessions use refund or rollback
safeguards to avoid silently losing committed resources.
[/list]

[h2]Economy[/h2]

[list]
[*] Dark Web prices were rebalanced to keep rare weapons, ammunition, generators,
armor, books and specialist magazines meaningful late-game purchases.
[*] Request prices and easy contract rewards were rebalanced to prevent rapid
access to top-tier equipment.
[*] Trading now uses one shared server market and charges a 5% fee on purchases
and sales.
[*] Every ATM receives a persistent cash reserve between $10,000 and $50,000,
restocking slowly over time so withdrawals alone can never permanently
drain a machine. The single ATM targeted by the Refill an ATM contract is
a smaller branch machine that starts nearly empty instead, so refilling
it actually matters.
[*] Negative reputation increases Dark Web and Request prices, while sustained
positive reputation grants a limited discount.
[*] Player-facing prices now use realistic configurable rounding tiers. Purchases
round upward, Dark Web resale rounds downward and contract rewards round to
the nearest tier.
[/list]

[h2]Contracts And Deliveries[/h2]

[list]
[*] The weekly contract board is shared by the server. Accepting an offer removes
it for everyone until the next board generation.
[*] All 13 contract types use canonical server state and persistent objective IDs.
[*] Kill contracts display live progress in their inventory note.
[*] Active contracts resynchronize after reconnecting, closing the computer and
interacting with relevant world context menus.
[*] Package, cargo, manhunt, protection, ATM refill and vehicle destinations now
use shared, city-aware Build 42.20 location pools.
[*] Package destinations are also reused for ammunition and medical mail drops.
[*] Refill an ATM sends the player to withdraw physical cash from ATMs around
town and carry it to one fixed target machine, requiring enough bank
balance to accept the contract in the first place.
[*] Elevated and underground objectives grant 5% additional contract reward per
absolute Z level.
[/list]

[h2]Interface And Accessibility[/h2]

[list]
[*] Poker and Blackjack use compact CRT-sized table layouts with readable card
ranks and two-color suits.
[*] Poker displays the player's current hand and an estimated win percentage
without exposing hidden AI cards or AI difficulty.
[*] Request conversations support wrapping, conditional scrolling and automatic
scrolling to the newest message.
[*] Contracts, Requests, login and ATM authentication share a UTF-8-safe typing
system with consistent human dialogue delays.
[*] Obsolete per-interface SFX controls were removed in favor of Project Zomboid's
global audio settings.
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]

PZLinux targets Project Zomboid Build 42.20 Stable and newer Build 42 versions.
It retains the B42_PZLinux mod ID and existing player/world ModData for save
compatibility. No additional mod dependency is required.

[h2]Known Limitations[/h2]

[list]
[*] Full controller navigation is not implemented; keyboard and mouse are
currently required.
[*] Split-screen is not officially supported or certified for 1.0.0.
[*] The server-authoritative architecture has automated coverage and hosted-server
live testing, but the final two-client dedicated-server restart/reconnect test
campaign remains required before claiming broad public-server certification.
[*] Translation catalogs exist for 20 locales. Afrikaans, Catalan and Vietnamese
catalogs, plus community proofreading of generated translations, remain to
be completed.
[/list]
